## Do's

1.	 Curate your public profile. Keep your profiles either professional or private, knowing that your peers, professors, and potential employers could view them.

2.	Verify all information before sharing. Fact check news and other sources to avoid spreading any sort of misinformation.
	
3.	Make use of privacy settings wisely. Review who can see your posts, stories, and personal information across all platforms.
	
4.	Maintain a respectful and formal tone online. Treat online discussions like physical conversations when engaging with faculty, administrators, or potential employers.
	
5.	Take regular digital breaks. Set a limit to screen time, prioritize sleep and physical as well as  protect your mental health from comparison culture.

## Don'ts

1.	Don't post online in moments of strong emotion. Avoid oversharing and venting about people you know personally because impulsive posts may damage relationships and reputations.
	
2.	Avoid sharing your location (real time location). Posting your exact whereabouts may compromise your physical safety.
	
3.	Refrain from engaging with trolls or toxic commenters. Getting into public arguments is useless and can lead to ruining your online reputation.
	
4.	Don't post confidential or dishonest content.
	
5.	Don't post under the influence of alcohol or any substance that may impair your judgment, this may lead to posts affecting your reputation or you may end up facing professional consequences.
