## Do's

1. Do curate your public profile. Keep your profiles professional or private, knowing that peers, professors, and potential employers may view them.

2. Do verify information before sharing. Take a moment to fact-check news and sources to avoid spreading misinformation or hoaxes.

3. Do use privacy settings wisely. Regularly review who can see your posts, stories, and personal information across all platforms.

4. Do maintain a respectful tone. Treat online discussions like in-person conversations, especially when engaging with faculty, administrators, or potential employers.

5. Do take regular digital breaks. Set boundaries to limit screen time, prioritize sleep, and protect your mental health from comparison culture.

## Don'ts

1. Don't post in moments of strong emotion. Avoid venting about professors, classmates, or employers online, as impulsive posts can damage relationships and reputations.

2. Don't share your real-time location. Posting your exact whereabouts (e.g., "out of town for the weekend") can compromise your physical safety and property security.

3. Don't engage with trolls or toxic commenters. Getting into public arguments rarely ends well and can reflect poorly on your judgment.

4. Don't post confidential or academically dishonest content. Never share exam content, another student's work, or proprietary information from internships or group projects.

5. Don't post under the influence. Alcohol or substances can impair judgment, leading to posts that may later result in academic or professional consequences.
